### Decision: REFERENCE (not ADOPT, not pure REJECT)
Reason: no license; every score tuned on the case it claims to validate; GDELT anchor is batch time and the key Maduro anchor is hand-typed. Reuse only the idea of fixed-window same-side co-occurrence edges (re-implemented by us with a null model) and the minute-share/HHI features, labelled exploratory. A score is never evidence of insider trading.

## Part 2: first public information timestamps (Problem 3)

### Five distinct clocks (never merge)
1. Event time (operation start). 2. First verifiable publication (a signed/official post or a first archived copy, an upper bound). 3. Aggregator indexing (GDELT batch, Wayback capture). 4. First market price move (CLOB trades). 5. First trade by an account.
Wayback capture and GDELT time are both >= publication time. They bound publication from above only.

### What each method can and cannot establish
| Method | Establishes | Cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| GDELT DOC 2.0 `seendate` | Crawl-batch time (15 min) of a URL, last ~3 months only | Publication time; anything older than ~3 months |
| GDELT GKG/Events/Mentions files (V2.1DATE, DATEADDED, MentionTimeDate) | Upper bound: item indexed by that 15-min batch; historical, free | Publication or event time; 7-37 min lag observed; sampling gaps (a story absent from a batch is not absent from the web) |
| Wayback CDX earliest capture | Upper bound on publication for a specific URL (here 06:45:28) | First publication; depends on crawl triggers; official X/Telegram often uncaptured; captured page date is the crawler's, but page body timestamps are publisher-controlled |
| Publisher markup (htmldate) | Claimed publication date, day resolution by default | Verified time; forgeable, local TZ, edits |
| Official post with its platform timestamp (Telegram, X, whitehouse.gov) | Strongest primary evidence if retrieved from platform or from a signed/archived copy | Needs independent archive to rule out later edit; some timestamps are display-localised |
Rule for the timeline: report "first verifiable publication <= T" with the method; never write "first public information at T" from GDELT alone. The 06:23 Israeli announcement (our verified item) is earlier than any aggregator trace found (06:45 Wayback, 07:00 GDELT), so aggregators can only corroborate, not date it.

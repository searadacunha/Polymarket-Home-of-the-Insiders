No code, table, threshold or test changes. `tables/` had been written by the frozen code and had not been opened when this was written. Three blind audits (`audit/AUDIT_SUMMARY.md`) found problems in the frozen design. Fixing them changes the method, so that is Benjamin's decision, and it would be a new study. This amendment only fixes what can be said.

## If a window returns PREDICTIVE_TIMING_PATTERN

The label is an output of the frozen code. It is reported, and it is not an interpretation. The allowed sentence is: at nominal 0.05/6, matched bursts beat entrants of the same cell in that window, as an association conditioned on a look-ahead key. Not allowed: "predictive" as a claim, "beyond public news", "private information", "detector", "capturable", "across events" without saying how many events carry it. Reasons:

- The combined news-day trap passed once, at 300 s, against the plan's own "must fail all four".
- H1 alone gave a positive result in 6 of 24 news-day runs, so matching does not stop that confound. H2 stopped 5 of them through an artefact at long windows.
- `total` descends from the outcome: it counts only entrants inside the price band, so a rising token stops adding.
- A burst's reference price is the VWAP up to its trigger, not the price at its trigger, so a rising price gives positive excess with no private information.
- The universe is filtered on lifetime volume, read after resolution.
- Clusters are events, while one world fact spans many events.

Adjacent windows passing together count as one fact. They are nested, not replications.

## If a window returns NOT_SUPPORTED

The allowed sentence is: no large average excess was found among matched bursts. Not allowed: "no effect", "no alignment", "refuted". Reasons:

- Medium synthetic effects of +0.10 were missed in 12 of 12 runs, and strong effects at 3600 s in 2 of 3.
- An auditor's simulation, on an assumed shape, gives about 6 to 8% power at +0.10 and 46 to 59% at +0.25. It rules out little below about +0.40 on all bursts.
- `total` can also hide an informed burst followed by a band exit, and rows just before an upward exit stay in the base.
- H2 is not monotone at long windows because of the cooldown. A failure of H2 at 900 or 3600 seconds is not evidence that wallets do not align.

## In every case

- The register's full count, 114, cannot be reached as coded: the floors are 0.0005 for H1 and 0.002 for H2, against 0.05/114 = 0.00044. This is a limit of resolution, and p at the floor is a bound, not an estimate.
- The controls did not fully pass. "Controls passed" is not to be written.
- `no_shock` does not clear public news: a first reaction to news counts as no shock.
- Strata with few bursts are not citable. The 75 cells lose bursts unevenly, and no minimum share is set.
- H2's real burst counts and analytic expected counts were seen before the freeze, so H2 is not a blind result.
- A row at the same second can enter `crowd`, against the plan's "earlier rows only".
- The catalogue ranks are exploratory, on 3 of 6 markets, one of them a capped file.

## Proposed for a second study, Benjamin's decision

Store the trigger-row price. Replace `total` by a key that does not descend from the outcome, with a synthetic band-exit path. Cluster on series as well as events, and add a leave-one-event-out check. Set a matched-share floor. Fix H2's interaction with the cooldown, and control all six windows. Use at least 200,000 draws if the full count is to be met. Measure the volume filter against a market sample below $250,000.
